Pay & benefits

What the job actually comes with

Landscaping has a reputation for cash-under-the-table and no-shows in January. None of that here.

01 / PAY

Weekly pay, every Friday

Direct deposit, overtime after 40, and a written wage review at 90 days and each March.

02 / WINTER

Guaranteed winter hours

Year-round crew get a minimum of 30 paid hours a week from December through February, snow or no snow.

03 / HEALTH

Health, dental, vision

Company covers 75% of the employee premium. Coverage starts the first of the month after 60 days.

04 / RETIREMENT

401(k) with 4% match

Fully vested at two years. Enrollment help from a real person, not just a packet.

05 / TRAINING

Certifications on the clock

ICPI, backflow, pesticide applicator, CDL. We pay the course, the exam, and your hours.

06 / GEAR

Boots, gear, and a $250 tool credit

Annual boot stipend, company-issued rain and cold-weather gear, hearing and eye protection stocked on every truck.

How hiring works

Four steps, about a week

STEP 1

Send the short form

Name, phone, what you have done before. Three minutes, no resume needed for crew roles.

STEP 2

Phone call within 2 days

Fifteen minutes with the hiring foreman. Wage, schedule, and start date on that first call.

STEP 3

Ride along, paid

Half a day on a real crew at full hourly rate. You see the work, the crew sees you.

STEP 4

Offer and start

Written offer the same week. Most new hires start the following Monday.

Do I need experience to get hired?

Not for crew member or seasonal grounds roles. About a third of our current foremen started with no landscape experience at all. What we need is reliable transportation, a willingness to work outside in the weather, and showing up when you said you would.

What time does the day start and end?

Shop at 6:30 a.m., trucks roll at 7:00. Most crews are back at the shop between 3:30 and 4:30. In July we start earlier to stay ahead of the heat.

Is the work year-round or seasonal?

Both, and you choose. Year-round roles carry a guaranteed 30-hour winter minimum plus snow call-outs. Seasonal runs March through November with an end-of-season bonus and a standing offer to convert.

Se habla español?

Sí. Cada cuadrilla tiene un capataz bilingüe, y las juntas de seguridad, la capacitación y los documentos de nómina están disponibles en español e inglés.
