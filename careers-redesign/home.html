Services

Four things, done properly

We turn down work outside these four. It is why the crews are good at what stays.

01 Landscape design

A measured site plan, a plant list with mature sizes, and a phased budget you can build over one season or three.

  • Site survey and grade check
  • Planting plan by sun and soil
  • Phased cost breakdown

02 Hardscape construction

Patios, walkways, seat walls, steps and retaining walls. Compacted base, proper drainage, no settling in year two.

  • Segmental and natural stone walls
  • Permeable and standard pavers
  • Drainage and grading correction

03 Planting & lawns

Trees, shrubs and perennials chosen for the site rather than the truck. Sod, seeding and soil amendment.

  • Nursery stock, tagged before delivery
  • Sod and overseeding
  • Bed edging and mulch

04 Maintenance & irrigation

Weekly or biweekly care, spring and fall cleanups, irrigation start-ups and winterizing, and snow service.

  • Scheduled maintenance routes
  • Irrigation service and repair
  • Winter snow and ice management

Process

From first call to first mow

PHASE 1

Site visit

We walk the property, take measurements, and talk about how you actually use the yard.

Week 1 · free
PHASE 2

Plan & estimate

A drawn plan, a plant list, and a line-item price. Two rounds of revisions included.

Weeks 2–3
PHASE 3

Schedule & permits

Utility locates, any needed permits, and a start date you get in writing.

Weeks 4–6
PHASE 4

Build

One crew, one foreman, start to finish. Daily cleanup, and a text update each evening.

1–3 weeks on site
PHASE 5

Walkthrough & care

Punch list closed out, watering schedule handed over, and a maintenance plan if you want one.
